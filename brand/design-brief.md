# sraww: logo design brief

**Status:** approved (Gate A, 2026-11-23). Round 1 concepts are in `brand/concepts/round-1/`. Gate C (app asset changes) is not granted.
**Sources:** your questionnaire answers in this session, plus project evidence from `package.json`, `index.html`, `README.md`, `assets/icon.svg` and `src/client/styles.css`.

## 1. Identity

- **Name:** `sraww`, exactly as in `package.json` and the README. Lowercase in the wordmark.
- **Tagline (recommended):** "Sketch the systems you build."
  - Alternatives: "Calm diagrams for your code." · "See how your code fits together."
- **Audience:** you first. Developers who may use it if it is open-sourced.
- **Personality:** premium, technical, minimal, with a touch of playfulness.
- **Feeling to create:** calm, and a sense of appreciation.

## 2. Visual direction

- **Aesthetic:** minimalist as the base. Geometric structure carries the mark. Brutalist traits (hard edges, stark contrast) are allowed only as accents.
- **Metaphors to explore (pick up to two per concept):**
  - two nodes joined by one path (architecture links)
  - a frame left open at one corner (a canvas or whiteboard)
  - one stroke with a slight hand-drawn quality (echoes the app's hand-drawn mode)
- **Avoid:** cartoon or mascot forms, pencils, gradient blobs, glossy 3D, and the generic "S" lettermark (the current mark is a placeholder).

## 3. Composition

- **Logo type:** a combination of symbol and wordmark. The symbol must also work alone as the app icon and favicon.
- **Geometry:** your answer asked for one organic and one angular. I will read this as: concept A is angular, concept B is organic, and concept C is free to mix the two. Please confirm (open question 3).
- **Balance:** asymmetric, centred on an optical axis. This suits the node-and-link idea.
- **Detail:** simple. The symbol must stay readable at 16 px.

## 4. Colour

- **Neutrals carry the identity:** near-black ink and warm off-white paper, both taken from the app's tokens (`--ink`, `--paper`).
- **One accent.** The current accent is an indigo family: `oklch(49% 0.22 275)` on light, `oklch(72% 0.17 275)` on dark. I recommend keeping it for continuity. Other palettes will be proposed with the concepts after approval.
- **Backgrounds:** the mark must work on paper, ink, pure white and pure black. It uses one colour per version, with no gradients.

## 5. Typography

- **Wordmark:** a geometric sans, lowercase, medium weight, slightly tight tracking. It will be drawn as outlines in SVG, so the files carry no font dependency.
- **Candidate typefaces (all open licence, SIL OFL):** Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Sans, Manrope. The licence will be checked before final use.
- **App UI:** unchanged. The wordmark can differ from the app's serif display face.

## 6. Usage

- Browser tab favicon and app header.
- README and documentation.
- Social avatar.
- The web app itself, which is the only website for now.

## 8. Constraints

- No copying of competitor marks or third-party logos. Excalidraw is a reference for style only.
- No changes to app assets, UI code, `index.html`, or the README until you give explicit permission (Gate C). That includes the current `assets/icon.svg`, which the favicon and README use.
- No git actions (commit, push, tag, PR).
